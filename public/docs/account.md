# Accounts, saving and plans

> How saving works in Stutr, signing in, exporting or deleting your data, the Free, Pro, Student, Classroom and Founder plans, and managing billing.

## Saving your work

- **Without an account**, your latest circuit and your projects are kept in this browser only.
- **With an account**, they are stored online and follow you to any device. Sign in with GitHub or Google from the login page.
- **Autosave** keeps the latest circuit so the editor reopens where you left off. You can turn it off in Settings.
- **Projects** are named copies you save on purpose. Use the Projects panel to save, open or delete them.
- **Export code** downloads `circuit.stutr.txt`. **Settings, Export my data** downloads everything you have saved as a file.
- Settings also lets you delete all your saved data. This cannot be undone.

See the [privacy policy](/privacy) for what is stored and why.

## Plans

Stutr is free to use. Paid plans add convenience and, as they are built, more features. See [Pricing](/pricing) for current prices.

| Plan | For | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | Learning and building | Every part, the code editor and canvas in sync, up to 5 saved projects (in your browser or your account), LED screens up to 64 by 64, and the example circuits. |
| Pro | Serious builders | Everything in Free plus unlimited projects, your own circuits as parts, LED screens up to 2048 by 2048, version history, export to PNG, netlist and Verilog, extra themes, and early access to Hardware Lab when it launches. |
| Student | Students with a school email | The same as Pro at a lower price. Needs an account whose email ends in .edu, .edu.xx or .ac.xx. |
| Classroom | Teachers and schools | Class tools for many students. Coming soon. |
| Founder | Early supporters | Pro for life, paid once. |

Limits apply only to the Free plan. If a paid plan ends, your circuits and projects stay yours: you can still open, run and export the code of everything you made.

## Your profile and plan

Open the menu and choose Profile and plan, or click your name at the bottom of the menu. You will see your account, your current plan and its status:

- **Active**: your plan is on, with the date it renews.
- **Ends on a date**: you cancelled, and your access continues until that date.
- **Lifetime**: the Founder plan has no renewal.
- **Free**: no paid plan yet, with a link to the plans.

The **Manage billing** button opens a secure page from our payment provider, Polar. There you can change your plan between monthly and yearly, cancel, update your card and download invoices. Changes appear in your profile within a minute.
